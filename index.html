    How ModuRank works

    Slide the dials, the map and ranking update live. Click a state to drill into its cities. Click cities (or use the Pin button) to compare them side-by-side.

    Scoring

    For each module, every state's value is min-max normalized into 0–1 (1 = best in the country, 0 = worst). Your weighted score is the weighted average of those normalized values across modules you have turned on. Map colors are rank-based — the top-ranked state is full green regardless of the absolute score gap.

    City scoring

    Cities are scored the same way. Modules with their own per-city data (income, rent, home value, education, climate, air quality) use the city's actual value. Modules with state-only data (taxes, gun friendliness, life expectancy, etc.) inherit from the city's state — marked (state) in the tooltip. Every city ends up scored across every module.

    Data sources

    • Census Bureau ACS 5-year — income, home value, rent, population, density, age, education, broadband, commute, uninsured (state + city level)
    • BLS LAUS — unemployment rate (state)
    • Open-Meteo — temperature, feels-like, humidity, sunshine, precipitation (state capital + per-city grid)
    • OpenWeather Air Pollution — PM2.5 air quality (per-city grid)
    • Redfin Data Center — median sale price, days on market, $/sqft, inventory (state)
    • CDC NCHS 2021 — life expectancy (state)
    • FBI UCR 2022 — violent crime rate (state; FL/MD/PA omitted due to NIBRS non-reporting)
    • Tax Foundation 2023 — effective property tax rate (state)
    • World Population Review / FEMA — cost of living, sales tax, income tax, home insurance, public lands, humidity, UV exposure, natural disasters (state)
    • GeoNames US bulk dump (CC-BY) — 155k US incorporated places with lat/lon and population
    • us-atlas / d3 — state polygons; Leaflet + Carto + Esri — base tiles

    Direction flip

    Each slider has a small ↑/↓ button. By default each module has a "natural" better direction (lower taxes = better, higher income = better). Click the arrow to flip — e.g. if you want more humidity instead of less, click Humidity's ↓ → becomes ↑.

    Privacy / runtime

    Everything runs in your browser. The SQLite databases are downloaded once (~250 KB up front, +60 MB lazily when you focus your first state) and cached normally. No API calls at runtime; no analytics; no tracking. Pin and theme state lives in your browser's localStorage.
